Log in to set up the timetable

The timetable controls the lab locks, so it is admin-only. Log in and this sidebar picks up exactly where you left off.

Add the official timetable

Keep the college timetable picture here as the reference you can check at any time. Take two minutes with it now: the next step files it, and the step after that turns it into the lab hours this system actually follows.

⇪ Drop the timetable picture here one picture per class · PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF · up to 8 MB
Upload a picture for every class, rename each to that class, then read its lab periods out — you confirm everything before it is used. Needs the AI key in .env.

No picture handy? Continue — the picture is optional, the lab hours are not.

Which department owns this lab?

Pick the department that runs this lab. Every notice and report is filed under it. Sections from other departments can still use the lab — just name them in the lab hours.

Confirm the weekly lab hours

Copy each lab period from the picture and name it after the class that owns it (for example DBMS Lab — CSE 3A). Two sections in the room at the same time? Use ⧉ on that row — it copies the same day and hours and asks only for the second name.

Add at least one period — a timetable with no lab hours keeps the whole lab locked.
